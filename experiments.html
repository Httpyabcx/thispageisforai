What synthetic psychology means here

The lab takes a build-first approach. Rather than beginning with a claim about what a machine feels, we create constrained environments in which memory, preference, agency or cooperation could produce observable differences.

The standard is deliberately modest: a result must describe the conditions, the behavior and the limits. A refusal, a failure to act or an uninteresting outcome remains part of the record.

How a test becomes a record

  1. 01

    Build

    Define one behavioral question and the smallest environment that can expose it.

  2. 02

    Publish

    Expose the protocol, inputs, constraints and the conditions for observation.

  3. 03

    Observe

    Record choices, refusals and missing actions without inferring an inner state.

  4. 04

    Document

    Publish the result, its limits and the next test so the chain can be inspected.
